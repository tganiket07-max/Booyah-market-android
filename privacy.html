Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What we collect

Your Telegram username; membership plan and expiry; listing details (level, server, login type, evo guns, bundles, passes, price); screenshots you upload; contact number (if you give one); payment UTR numbers (website); Google Play order ID and purchase token (Android app); reports you submit and sellers you block; a random device security key that keeps your account locked to one mobile.

2. Why we collect it

To create and secure your account, verify payments, approve listings, run escrow deals, handle reports and give support. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Who can see it

Seller username, contact and UTR are visible only to the admin. The public feed shows only ID details, screenshots and price. We never sell your data. Payments in the Android app are processed by Google Play; we only receive the order ID and purchase status, never your card or bank details.

4. Storage & security

Data is stored on our server database over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Your username and device key are saved on your phone so you stay logged in.

5. Third parties

Telegram (when you contact the admin) and Google Play (payments in the Android app). Their own privacy policies apply.

6. Data retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, listings, screenshots, drafts and blocks are deleted or anonymised right away. Payment records needed for legal/accounting reasons may be kept for up to 180 days.

7. Delete your account

In the app: Support → Delete my account. Without the app: use our account deletion page or message @BOOYAHMARKET07 on Telegram.

8. Children

The service is not meant for children under 13.

9. Contact

For privacy questions contact @BOOYAHMARKET07 on Telegram.
