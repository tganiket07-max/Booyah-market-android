Delete your BOOYAH MARKET account

App: BOOYAH MARKET · Developer: BOOYAH MARKET (@BOOYAHMARKET07)

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

Open the app → Support → My Account → Delete my account → type your username to confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request here

If you no longer have the app or phone, send a request. Admin will verify you on Telegram and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your profile and username, membership, all your listings and screenshots, drafts, reports you made and sellers you blocked. Your username becomes free for any device.

What may be kept

Payment records (Google Play order ID / UTR, amount, date) may be kept for up to 180 days for legal, accounting and fraud-prevention reasons, then deleted.
