📜 Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026

1. Eligibility

You must be 18+ (or have permission from a parent/guardian) and use your own real Telegram username. One account per person.

2. One account, one mobile

For security, a username can be logged in on only one mobile/device at a time. To use it on another device, first delete the account from the current device (Support → Delete my account), or ask the admin on Telegram to reset the device lock after verifying you.

3. Listings

Sellers must be the real owner of every Free Fire ID they list. All details, screenshots, level, bundles and evo guns must be true. Fake, stolen, hacked or banned IDs are strictly prohibited and lead to a permanent block.

4. Fees & Commission

Regular/VIP sellers pay a ₹25 listing fee + 10% commission on a successful sale. VIP PRO members pay 0% commission and no listing fee during their active plan. In the Android app, listing fees, memberships and escrow are paid through Google Play Billing; on the website they may be paid by UPI. Fees and memberships are non-refundable once the listing is approved or the plan is activated, except where required by law or by Google Play's refund policy.

5. Admin Approval

Regular/VIP listings go live only after admin verifies the payment and details. Admin may reject, delete or mark any listing as SOLD at any time.

6. Buying & Escrow

All deals must go through the BOOYAH MARKET admin on Telegram. Never pay a seller directly outside the platform — we are not responsible for losses from outside deals.

7. Account Handover

After payment, the seller must hand over full login access (Google/Facebook/VK/X) and must not try to recover the ID later. Recovery attempts result in a permanent ban and loss of payment.

8. Reporting & Blocking

Every listing has a Report button and a Block seller option. Admin reviews reports and may delete listings or block users who break these terms.

9. Prohibited Use

Fraud, fake UTRs or payments, spam, abusive behaviour or impersonating the admin lead to an immediate block without refund.

10. Disclaimer

BOOYAH MARKET is an independent marketplace and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Garena, Free Fire or Google. Trading game accounts may be against the game publisher's rules; users trade at their own risk.

11. Account deletion

You can delete your account anytime from Support → Delete my account in the app, or request deletion on our account deletion page.

12. Changes

We may update these terms. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated terms.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What we collect

Your Telegram username; membership plan and expiry; listing details (level, server, login type, evo guns, bundles, passes, price); screenshots you upload; contact number (if you give one); payment UTR numbers (website); Google Play order ID and purchase token (Android app); reports you submit and sellers you block; a random device security key that keeps your account locked to one mobile.

2. Why we collect it

To create and secure your account, verify payments, approve listings, run escrow deals, handle reports and give support. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Who can see it

Seller username, contact and UTR are visible only to the admin. The public feed shows only ID details, screenshots and price. We never sell your data. Payments in the Android app are processed by Google Play; we only receive the order ID and purchase status, never your card or bank details.

4. Storage & security

Data is stored on our server database over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Your username and device key are saved on your phone so you stay logged in.

5. Third parties

Telegram (when you contact the admin) and Google Play (payments in the Android app). Their own privacy policies apply.

6. Data retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, listings, screenshots, drafts and blocks are deleted or anonymised right away. Payment records needed for legal/accounting reasons may be kept for up to 180 days.

7. Delete your account

In the app: Support → Delete my account. Without the app: use our account deletion page or message @BOOYAHMARKET07 on Telegram.

8. Children

The service is not meant for children under 13.

9. Contact

For privacy questions contact @BOOYAHMARKET07 on Telegram.
